What We Do

Bespoke aviation services,
crafted around you.

From a single charter to a full aircraft acquisition, we bring deep expertise and genuine independence to every brief.

Private Jet Charter

On-demand charter worldwide, sourced from the open market and safety-vetted for every departure. We match you with the right aircraft at the right price — with no fleet bias, ever.

Aircraft Sales & Acquisition

Searching for your next aircraft, or ready to sell? Our team navigates the global pre-owned market — from brief and market analysis through pre-purchase inspection, title, and closing.

Jet Card & Membership Programs

Frequent flyers deserve more certainty. We advise on and arrange jet-card and subscription programs that fit your travel profile — guaranteed availability, fixed pricing, simplified booking.

Advisory & Aircraft Management

For owners seeking expert oversight — we provide impartial advice on fleet strategy, operator selection, charter revenue optimisation, and ongoing aircraft management relationships.

The Habibi Difference

We represent you,
not a fleet.

Habibi Aviation Partners is an independent, global brokerage — we own no aircraft. That independence is our most important asset. Every recommendation is driven purely by what is right for your mission, your safety requirements, and your budget — never by a fleet to fill.

With strong roots across the Gulf, Middle East, Europe, and beyond, we are more than a booking service: we are a long-term relationship. Your advisor learns your preferences, your standards, and your schedule — and is available whenever you need them.

The Process

From brief to boarding
in three steps.

We handle every detail so you can focus on the journey.

01

Tell Us Your Trip

Share your route, dates, group size, and any preferences. Your advisor takes a full brief — no detail is too small when it comes to your comfort and safety.

02

We Source & Vet

We canvas the open market, evaluate operator safety records, compare pricing, and present you with the best-matched options — transparently, with our honest recommendation.

03

You Fly

We handle all logistics, documentation, and ground coordination. Your only task is to arrive at the aircraft and enjoy the journey. We remain available throughout.
